Accordingly, there are no broker non-votes.)</td></tr><tr><td>Stockholder proposal to permit shareholder action by written consent.</td><td>Majority of votes present in person or by proxy at the meeting must be cast for the proposal.</td><td>Abstentions and broker non-votes have the same effect as votes against.</td></tr><tr><td>Any other matter that may properly be submitted at the meeting.</td><td>Majority of votes present in person or by proxy at the meeting must be cast for the proposal.</td><td>Abstentions and broker non-votes have the same effect as votes against.</td></tr></table>
